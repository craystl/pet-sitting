About Us

Welcome to Chirpal's Pet Sitting! We are a small, friendly business located in Hamilton, providing reliable, gentle, and compassionate care to furry and feathered friends. We understand that leaving your beloved pet in someone else's care can sometimes feel worrying, which is why we strive to create a safe, comfortable, and welcoming environment for every animal that stays with us. We're honored to take care of your pet and can't wait to introduce them to their temporary home at our pet care centre.

Our team consists of trained professionals who are knowledgeable and experienced in caring for the needs of dogs, cats, birds, and many other types of pets. We take the time to get to know each animal individually, making sure their routines, personalities, and needs are respected throughout their stay. During booking, please include any special requirements, dietary needs, medications, or other information your pet may require.

Whether you're going away for a short trip or need someone to look after your pet for a little longer, Chirpal's Pet Sitting is here to help. Thank you for choosing Chirpal's Pet Sitting! We look forward to meeting you and your wonderful pets!
